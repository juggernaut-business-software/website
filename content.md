Juggernaut is a business automation system that uses AI to automate almost 100% of business processes.

Imagine hiring business consultants, a board of directors, executives, secretaries, an HR team, a PR team, a Marketing team, business intelligence and data analysts, and subscribing to ERP software for your business. You can do all of this at a cost less than that of buying ERP software now.

## Workhorse

Imagine a team of professionals who work continuously without rest; that's Juggernaut, an AI that works tirelessly for your business.

## Listener

Listening and observing are essential parts of intelligence growth. Juggernaut listens to you, asks questions, and learns about your business.

## Learner

Juggernaut learns, like a baby learns; it goes through the data it has, reads the data the best books have to offer, and keeps up with current trends.

## Researcher

Juggernaut researches, like a scientist; it uses its knowledge to solve problems, make predictions, and advance knowledge.

## Data Analyst

Juggernaut is a data analyst; it uses your business data and real-world data to give you instant insights into your business.

## Talker

Juggernaut talks, like a human; it uses its knowledge to communicate with you about your business, learns from you how its suggestions fared, and gives you new ideas with reasoning every day for growth.

## Salesman

Juggernaut sells like a salesman; it uses its knowledge to sell products, services, and solutions to customers. It tirelessly finds potential customers and sells them products, services, and solutions.

## HR

Managing people, payroll, recruitment, salary negotiations, checking on human efficiency, their holiday records, letting people go, counselling people—these are tough. Why not let Juggernaut do it for you? Yes, Juggernaut can do it for you, with almost zero effort.

## Librarian

Juggernaut is a librarian; it stores every business document of yours and retrieves it lightning fast.

## Software Developer

Juggernaut can learn about your business and write highly customized software for it, thus creating an ERP on its own.

## Wise Guy

As time progresses, Juggernaut becomes a wise guy; it uses its knowledge to make decisions, solve problems, and advance knowledge. It advises you and your teams on how to run your business, and it runs it all by itself if you wish.

## Saves A Lot

Juggernaut saves you a lot of time, effort, and money; it automates all your business processes, so you can focus on what matters most. It does all this at a price lower than that of ERP software.
